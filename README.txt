HAMZA IRAFAAAN — PORTFOLIO SITE (CINEMATIC 3D MOTION EDITION)
==================================================================

An exact architectural & motion graphics adaptation inspired by riyanhafeez.netlify.app, customized for Hamza Irafaaan (Video Editor, Motion Designer & Logo Animator).

Files:
- index.html        : Semantic markup, accessible navigation, 3D carousel container, video showcase, and lightbox modal.
- assets/style.css  : Dark-mode design system, keyframes, starry ambient lighting, 3D perspective, responsive breakpoints.
- assets/script.js  : 3D curved slider with drag physics, scroll progress, tilt effects, magnetic buttons, spotlight shaders, and video lightbox.

Included Features & Motion Graphics:
1. Preloader curtain animation with monogram branding.
2. Floating fixed glassmorphism navbar that shrinks on scroll and tracks active sections.
3. 3D Curved Infinity Slider (Hero Carousel) with interactive pointer drag physics, velocity inertia, and 3D cylinder rotation.
4. Kinetic letter-by-letter animated hero headline.
5. High-contrast electric marquee ticker.
6. Featured video cards with 3D perspective mouse tilt, hover scale, and duration badges.
7. Interactive 9:16 / 16:9 Lightbox video player with keyboard and arrow navigation.
8. Selected work showcases with multi-thumbnail strip previews.
9. Interactive skill cards with animated percentage fill bars.
10. Work history timeline with radar-pulsing indicator dots and cubic-eased numerical stats counters.
11. Contact card spotlight shaders that dynamically track the mouse position.
12. One-click copy email button with clipboard confirmation.
13. Edge-to-edge giant footer typography reveal that dynamically auto-scales on viewport resize.
14. Ambient cursor glow tracker and magnetic attraction buttons.

How to Preview & Deploy:
- Local preview: Open index.html directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or any modern web browser.
- Netlify: Drag and drop the `hamza-portfolio` folder directly into the Netlify deploy dashboard.
- GitHub Pages: Push the contents to a repository and enable GitHub Pages in Settings > Pages.
